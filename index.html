About — Manifesto

Rooted.
Refined.
Unapologetically
Mexican.

Primitivo was born from a single belief: that Mexican cuisine deserves the same depth of attention given to any great culinary tradition. Behind the restaurant is a team of chefs, designers, musicians, and storytellers who share one standard: nothing without purpose.

Every dish carries the memory of a region, reinterpreted with precision and without apology. From the milpa to the pass — a table built for those who notice what others overlook.
